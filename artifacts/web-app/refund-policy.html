Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Returns

We accept returns within 48 hours of delivery, provided the item is unused, unworn, and in its original packaging with tags attached. Contact us on WhatsApp at +234 802 549 7647 to start a return.

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Earrings and other pierced jewelry, for hygiene reasons
  • Items marked as final sale
  • Items damaged due to customer misuse after delivery

3. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no extra cost to you.

4. Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval status. Approved refunds for online payments are processed back to your original Paystack payment method within 5–10 business days. Refunds for orders paid via bank transfer or on delivery are made directly to your bank account.

5. Exchanges

We're happy to exchange an item for a different size or colour, subject to availability, within the same 48-hour window.

6. How to Request a Return or Refund

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at +234 802 549 7647 with your order details
  2. Share photos of the item if it arrived damaged or incorrect
  3. We'll confirm pickup or drop-off arrangements
  4. Once received and inspected, we process your refund or exchange

7. Contact Us

Questions about a return? Reach us on WhatsApp at +234 802 549 7647 or by email at eghenovakate@gmail.com.
