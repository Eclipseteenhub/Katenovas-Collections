Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Delivery Coverage

We deliver across Nigeria — within Benin City and Edo State, as well as nationwide via trusted courier partners.

2. Delivery Timelines

  • Benin City / Edo State: 1–2 business days
  • Other states in Nigeria: 3–7 business days

Timelines are estimates and may vary due to courier delays, public holidays, or the customer's location.

3. Delivery Fees

Delivery fees depend on your location and the size of your order. The exact fee will be confirmed with you on WhatsApp or shown at checkout before you complete payment.

4. Order Processing

Orders are processed and prepared for dispatch within 24 hours of confirmation, Monday to Saturday. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed the next business day.

5. Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, we'll update you on its status via WhatsApp or phone. You can also check your order status by contacting us directly with your order reference.

6. Failed Delivery Attempts

Please ensure your delivery address and phone number are correct at checkout. If a delivery attempt fails due to an incorrect address or an unreachable customer, additional delivery charges may apply for a second attempt.

7. Contact Us

For delivery questions, reach us on WhatsApp at +234 802 549 7647 or by email at katenovascollections@gmail.com.
