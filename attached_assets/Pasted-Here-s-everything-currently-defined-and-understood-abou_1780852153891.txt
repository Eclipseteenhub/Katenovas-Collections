# Main Goal of the Website

Build a professional business website where customers can:

* View products
* View prices
* View images
* Search products
* Add products to cart
* Change quantities
* View total order summary
* Send orders through WhatsApp

At the same time, your mum should be able to:

* Login as admin
* Add products
* Edit products
* Delete products
* Upload product images

* Change stock status

The website should be:

* Fast
* Simple
* Mobile-first
* Beginner-friendly
* Easy to maintain
* Easy to upgrade later
