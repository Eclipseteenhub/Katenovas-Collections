# Smart Product Upload & Media Management System

## Objective

Build a modern, intuitive, and mobile-first product management system specifically designed for small businesses that primarily sell products through WhatsApp using videos rather than photos.

The goal is to eliminate the need for sellers to take separate product pictures. Instead, the application should fully support video-based product listings while still providing the polished appearance of a professional e-commerce website.

The experience should be simple enough that a non-technical business owner can upload products within minutes.

---

# Product Creation Workflow

The product creation process should guide the seller through a series of simple steps.

Each step should be visually clear, require minimal effort, and be optimized for mobile devices.

---

# Step 1 — Upload Product Media

The seller should be able to upload products in three different ways.

### Option 1 — Video Only

The seller uploads only a video.

The application automatically creates a temporary cover image by extracting a frame from the video.

No additional work is required.

This option is intended for businesses that already advertise products using WhatsApp videos.

---

### Option 2 — Image Only

The seller uploads only images.

This behaves like a traditional e-commerce product.

The uploaded image becomes the product cover.

---

### Option 3 — Video + Custom Cover Image

The seller uploads:

* Product Video
* Separate Product Image

The uploaded image becomes the official cover image while the video remains available for customers to watch.

This works similarly to YouTube where creators upload a custom thumbnail.

---

# Supported Media

Support common formats including:

Videos

* MP4
* MOV
* WEBM

Images

* JPG
* JPEG
* PNG
* WEBP

---

# Step 2 — Lightweight Built-in Video Editor

If the uploaded media contains a video, a lightweight editor should automatically open before the product is saved.

The editor should not attempt to compete with professional editing software.

Instead, it should provide only the tools that are genuinely useful for business owners.

---

## Feature 1 — Trim Video

The seller should be able to drag the beginning and end of the timeline.

Purpose:

Many sellers begin recording before the product is fully visible or continue recording after finishing.

This feature allows them to keep only the useful section.

Example

Original Video

2 minutes

Trimmed Video

37 seconds

Only the relevant product showcase remains.

---

## Feature 2 — Mute Audio

Include a simple toggle:

🔇 Remove Audio

Some videos contain:

* Background conversations
* Television sounds
* Music
* Environmental noise

Muting the audio should preserve the video while removing all sound.

---

## Feature 3 — Video Preview

Before saving, the seller should preview exactly what customers will see.

The preview should include:

* Trimmed video
* Muted audio (if selected)
* Final playback

---

## Feature 4 — Automatic Compression

After editing, the application should automatically compress the video while preserving visual quality.

Goals:

* Faster loading
* Reduced storage
* Better performance on mobile networks

Compression should happen automatically without requiring user interaction.

---

# Step 3 — Product Cover Selection

Every product requires a cover image.

The seller should have three options.

---

## Option A — Automatic Thumbnail

The system automatically extracts a clear frame from the video.

No action is required.

This serves as the default option.

---

## Option B — Choose Frame From Video

Provide a timeline of video frames.

The seller scrolls through the frames.

After finding the best angle, they tap:

Use This Frame

The selected frame becomes the product cover.

---

## Option C — Upload Custom Cover Image

Allow the seller to upload a completely separate image.

This behaves exactly like YouTube custom thumbnails.

The uploaded image replaces the automatically generated thumbnail.

This is useful when the seller already has a professionally taken product photo.

---

# Step 4 — Product Information

After media is finalized, collect product information.

Required Fields

* Product Name
* Price
* Category
* Description
* Quantity in Stock

Optional Fields

* Brand
* Discount
* Product Weight
* Available Colors
* Available Sizes
* Material
* Product Tags

---

# Step 5 — Save Product

When saving, the application stores:

Original Video

Edited Video

Cover Image

Product Details

Date Created

Seller Information

Inventory Information

The original upload should remain available internally in case the seller wants to edit the product later.

---

# Customer Experience

When customers browse products:

They first see the cover image.

If the product includes a video, display a small Play icon.

Clicking the product opens the product page.

Customers can:

View images

Watch the product video

Read the description

View price

Select variations

Add to cart

Share product

Contact seller through WhatsApp

---

# Future Feature — Multi-Product Creator

This feature is designed for sellers who record one long video containing multiple products.

Example

One three-minute video contains:

Handbag

Shoes

Necklace

Watch

Perfume

Instead of uploading five different videos, the seller uploads the recording once.

The application then allows them to repeatedly create product listings from different sections of the same video.

Workflow

Upload one video.

Select "Create Product From Clip."

Trim the section showing Product A.

Choose or upload a cover image.

Enter product details.

Save Product A.

Repeat the process for Product B, Product C, and so on.

The original uploaded video remains available until all desired products have been created.

Benefits

One recording session can generate multiple independent product listings.

This reduces upload time, storage usage, and repetitive work while matching how many WhatsApp businesses already record their products.

---

# Future AI Enhancements (Optional)

AI should never be required for the core workflow.

However, optional AI features can improve convenience.

Potential AI features include:

* Suggesting the clearest frame for the cover image.
* Detecting blurry or low-quality thumbnails.
* Recommending the best clip based on product visibility.
* Auto-generating short product descriptions from the seller's voice or captions.
* Automatically removing silent or irrelevant sections of the video.
* Background removal or enhancement for cover images.

These features should remain optional so the application works perfectly without AI.

---

# Design Principles

The entire experience should follow these principles:

* Mobile-first design.
* Beginner-friendly interface.
* Large touch-friendly controls.
* Minimal learning curve.
* Fast loading.
* Smooth animations.
* Clean, premium visual design.
* Professional e-commerce appearance.
* WhatsApp-inspired simplicity.
* Every action should require as few taps as possible.

The final experience should feel effortless for business owners while giving customers a polished, trustworthy, and engaging shopping experience.
